## What is still original

- The architecture shell: floor, wall, ceiling, landing and column boxes built by `scripts/level.gd` (skinned with the FoxTex textures above) and their invisible collision.
- Effects: blood and ember particles (small cubes), the ember projectile sphere, enemy blob shadows, muzzle flash lights.
- Text: room signs (Label3D) and the 2D interface.
- Synthesized sound effects in `assets/audio` (the library contains music only, no weapon or footstep effects).
